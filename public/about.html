Settle any argument.

VersusAI is a comparison game. You bring two things, an AI judge scores them, and the verdict is a percentage nobody can argue with. (They will anyway.)

Why it exists

Every group chat has the same fight: this or that. VersusAI turns the fight into a game where you commit to a side first and the AI reveals who actually wins.

What makes it fun

You pick before you know the score. The judge gives each side a percentage and a short case for and against. Then it tells you whether you called it right.

What you can compare

Anything with two sides: food, sports stars, phones, programming languages, cities, habits.
