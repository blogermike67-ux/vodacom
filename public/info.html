How it works

Four steps, about ten seconds a round.

  1. Type your two items

    Fill in the 1st item and the 2nd item boxes. Anything goes.

  2. Pick your side

    Press Pick 1 or Pick 2 to say who you think wins.

  3. Press AI Generate Compare

    The judge looks up a photo for each side, scores both, and writes a short case for each.

  4. See the verdict

    The higher percentage wins. If it's your pick, you win the round.

The outcome

You win

Your pick scored higher than the other side.

You lose

The judge preferred the other side. Try again.

Questions

Is the AI always right?

No. It gives an opinion based on what it knows, so treat the percentage as a fun verdict, not a fact.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the game and play.

What can I compare?

Any two things: foods, people, products, ideas. Keep it friendly.

Why did my photo not load?

Some words don't match any picture. Try a simpler or more common name.
